Frequently asked questions

  • Why don't my numbers match my paycheck exactly? The app models federal income tax only — it doesn't account for state tax, FICA, AMT, capital-loss netting, or a few other edge cases. It's an estimate, sharpened by the numbers you enter, not a substitute for a tax professional.
  • Where is my data stored? Entirely on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy for details.
  • My saved inputs disappeared after an update. If you're updating from an older build, a storage-format change can require re-entering your numbers once. This is called out in-app when it applies.
  • What's "pirate mode"? A fully optional easter egg that translates the app into pirate-speak. Turn it off before entering your numbers — "pillageable ordinary booty" is a lot less useful than "taxable ordinary income."
  • Is this tax advice? No. Tax Bracket Thermometer provides educational estimates only. For anything that affects your actual filing, please consult a qualified tax professional.

Reporting a bug

When emailing about a bug, it helps to include your iOS version, iPhone model, and a quick description of what you entered and what you expected to see. Screenshots are always welcome.
